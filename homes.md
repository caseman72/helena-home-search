## Methodology Notes

### Data Quality
- All data extracted from Zillow JSON feed dated late 2025
- Days on Market data unavailable for 10 listings (marked as N/A)
- One property (#51) is an auction listing with $0 price (excluded from price calculations)
- One property (#12) has unusual 432 sqft (likely cabin/outbuilding), excluded from certain analyses

### Calculation Methods
- Price per Square Foot = Price / Area
- Price per Acre = Price / Lot Size
- Composite Score weighted by: Price/SqFt (40%), Total Price (25%), Acreage (15%), DOM (10%), Functional (10%)

### Limitations
- Analysis based solely on listing data; condition, finishes, and location quality not assessed
- School district, crime rates, and neighborhood factors not included
- Market conditions may have changed since data collection
- Some listings may be pending or sold
