Montana winters are severe. Properties in the following locations may have challenging winter access:

- **Jefferson City (Wickes Rd, Ante Ln):** Higher elevation, potentially more snow
- **Clancy Area (Forest Park, Meadow Ln):** Mountain roads may be icy
- **Rimini Rd:** Western approach to Helena, known for winter weather
- **Colorado Gulch:** Elevated terrain

**Recommendation:** Verify road maintenance status and 4WD requirements for properties over 10 miles from Helena center during winter months.
